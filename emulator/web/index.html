What each jack accepts
Analog CV in (bipolar) — DRY, 1–8, SPREAD, TIME, FEEDBACK, HPF, LPF. Continuous voltage that modulates the param. TIME is the pitch input (≈ 1V/oct).
Gate / trigger in — the GATE jack. Digital on/off only: clock, sync, trigger. Cannot read analog pitch.
Audio in — IN L / IN R (line level; unused by Fox Tail).
Audio out — OUT L / OUT R (oscillator output).
