What each jack accepts
Analog CV in (bipolar) — continuous voltage summed into the panel control. Jacks still carry their delay-era legends: DRY and 1–8 are the nine slider CVs (inharmonicity, then the 8 bands); TIME is pitch (calibrated 1V/oct); SPREAD and FEEDBACK are the shaper window's start and width; HPF and LPF are the two per-mode shaper params.
Gate / trigger in — the GATE jack. Digital on/off only: clock, sync, trigger; cannot read analog pitch. High selects ODD ONLY, low (unpatched) is all partials.
Audio in — IN L / IN R (line level; unused by Fox Tail).
Audio out — OUT L / OUT R (oscillator output).

Sliders 2–9 are 8 geometric bands of a 96-partial bank: over their whole travel they set the band's level, and above half they also fill it in — so all sliders at half is a skeleton comb of eight partials. Pots 4–9 choose each band's fill order (CCW highpass → centre bandpass → CW lowpass). Pot 1 slides the whole spectral shape ±1 octave, pot 2 is fine tune, pot 3 is stereo spread. The four right-hand knobs drive the shaper — CLUSTER pulls partials toward their cluster start, SHEPARD slides them toward the next partial up. See docs/claude/control-maps.md.

Shaded band = shaper window (SPREAD/FEEDBACK). Dashed blue = one marker per slider — bands are geometric, ~0.8 octave each, not true octaves. Red dashes = Nyquist; partials past it fade out and show as faint ticks. Colour is pan: blue left, red right. Gaps in the comb are the sliders' fill and the pots' fill order, not missing partials — silent ones are still rendered.
